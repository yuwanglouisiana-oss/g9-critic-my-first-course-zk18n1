# Atoms

## Objectives

*What a student can do after this chapter (from the concept map).*

1. Find the number of protons, neutrons and electrons in a neutral atom from its mass number and atomic number.
2. Write the nuclide notation of an atom, such as $\ce{^{12}_{6}C}$, from its element, mass number and atomic number.

## First section

**Worked example: carbon-12.** Carbon-12 is written $\ce{^{12}_{6}C}$. The top number is the mass number (12) and the bottom number is the atomic number (6).

1. Protons = atomic number = **6**.
2. Neutrons = mass number − atomic number = 12 − 6 = **6**.
3. Electrons = protons in a neutral atom = **6**.

So carbon-12 has 6 protons, 6 neutrons and 6 electrons.

 An atom has a nucleus of protons and neutrons, surrounded by electrons. Water is $\ce{H2O}$ and $E = mc^2$.
